What this is

A learning plan for working better with Claude. Each card is a course, experiment, or habit, in suggested order.

How to use it

Drag a card or use Move to change its board. Choose Did not finish for cards that weren't useful or are gone, then pick a reason in its notes. Open Notes to write, and your notes save as you go. Use + on a board to add your own link, and switch between Rows and Columns above the board.
